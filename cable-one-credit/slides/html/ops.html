Cable One · credit view · Sources: Q1–Q3 2026 releases; Q3 10-Q

Run-rate free cash flow: Q3 EBITDA of $159.8m annualizes to $639m. Capex was $220m for nine months, about $295m a year. Interest was $39.4m gross in Q3; with the extra $200m revolver draw, call it $170m a year. So $639m less $295m less $170m is roughly $175m before tax. The decline is real: EBITDA down 21% year on year, revenue down 12%, and margins compressing as ARPU is used to defend subscribers. The Q3 subscriber improvement may be bought with price, so I would not underwrite a recovery in EBITDA. The point for credit is that this business does not need new money to operate; it needs its 2028 maturities dealt with.
