Cable One: own Term Loan B-4 at 83

A liquidity crisis with a mispriced balance sheet. At today’s loan prices the market values the business at ~2.8× EBITDA. B-4 is covered down to ~2.8×, or ~3.7× if $480m leaks to MBI, and the company’s own plan repays it at par.

Prepared by Sam

Figures from the Q3 2026 10-Q unless noted · loan prices as of Nov 19, 2026

Open with the call, then defend it. Cable One has not filed. It skipped a $10m coupon on Nov 16 and its 30-day grace ends around Dec 15–16, at which point the bank debt cross-defaults. So this is a "paths to and through a restructuring" pitch, not a post-bankruptcy one. B-4 trades at 83 and B-2/B-3 at 82. Pricing the loans there, the revolver at a similar level and the unsecured at their Sep 30 marks, the market values the business at about 2.8x Q3 run-rate EBITDA. B-4 only loses money below about 2.8x on $580m of EBITDA, or about 3.7x if $480m goes to MBI first. At 83 this is a carry trade with a free option, not a deep-value call: about 14% simple and 22% a year on expected timing including a par takeout in the company's own plan that costs only 1 point over B-2/B-3.
