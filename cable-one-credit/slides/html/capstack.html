Read the table bottom-up. The unsecured paper is $848m, last marked at 24 to 30 at Sep 30. The secured is $2.9bn, all pari: revolver and three term loans under one credit agreement. The term loans now trade at 82 to 83, up from the 10-Q's blended 77.8 mark at Sep 30, despite the missed coupon. B-4, which the company's own plan repays at par, trades only 1 point over B-2 and B-3. Two structural facts: first, notes and converts share guarantors with the bank debt, so the unsecured claim sits at the same entities, just behind the lien. Second, the loans pay only SOFR plus 200 to 225, which is off-market for a credit in this position, so part of the discount is coupon, not principal risk.
