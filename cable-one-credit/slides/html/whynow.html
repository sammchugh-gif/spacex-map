The timeline matters because the catalyst is dated. The company has said in the 10-Q that its liquidity cannot repay the 2028 debt, and it chose to skip a $10m coupon while holding roughly $675m of cash at Sep 30. That is a negotiating signal, not a cash problem: they want the unsecured holders and GTCR at the table. Under the indenture the coupon becomes an Event of Default only after 30 days, and the credit agreement's cross-default turns on that same grace expiry, so mid-December is when the bank lenders get remedies. The TRO hearing moved from Nov 13 to Dec 4. On the maturity wall: the springing maturity on B-2/B-3 means effectively all bank debt is due in 2028 unless B-4 is dealt with.
