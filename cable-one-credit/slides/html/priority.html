Cable One · credit view · Sources: Q3 10-Q note 8; MBI purchase agreement, Jan 3, 2026

Rank 1: the revolver and the three term loans sit under one credit agreement, all senior secured and pari; the 10-Q discloses no first-out revolver. The swaps would rank with them if a lender holds them, but they are currently an asset to Cable One, so there is no claim. Rank 2: the notes and converts are both senior unsecured, guaranteed by the subsidiaries that guarantee the bank debt, and pari with each other; trade claims sit alongside them. If the loans were ever undersecured, their shortfall would join this pool as a deficiency claim. On GTCR: I had argued its claim would be structurally junior because it sits only at the parent. The purchase agreement says otherwise. Cable One, Inc. signed it directly, alongside its wholly owned Major Merger Sub, and Cable One, Inc. is also the borrower and the notes' issuer. So a damages claim on rejection is pari with the notes at the issuer. The notes' only edge is their subsidiary guarantees, which matter for value held below the parent; the filings I have read don't show how assets split between the parent and its subsidiaries, so I keep GTCR pari in the waterfall. MBI's own $938m of debt sits in a separate silo, not guaranteed by Cable One.
