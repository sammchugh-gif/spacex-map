One trade, two passes. At 83, B-4 recovers about 89, 100, 100 and 73 across Bear, Base, Bull and Stress: about 93 weighted in Chapter 11. Adding the 22.5% chance of a par takeout out of court and interest until a filing, about 95 expected: 14% simple and about 22% a year on the expected timing. If the reorganised debt and equity are sold at emergence at 95 and a 30% discount, that falls to about 8% a year, so this is a carry trade, not a deep-value call, and I would size it that way. What makes B-4 the right tranche is the 1-point spread to B-2/B-3: for 1 point you get a par takeout if the company's GTCR plan goes ahead, and the same Chapter 11 claim if it doesn't. The notes are fairly priced, and the converts are the same claim at a higher price. The revolver, notes and converts prices are still Sep 30 marks; only the term loan prices are live.
