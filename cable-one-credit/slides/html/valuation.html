This is the "where is consensus wrong" slide. With the term loans at 82 to 83, the revolver assumed at 82, the notes and converts at their Sep 30 marks and the equity at about $30m, total claims less pro forma cash imply an EV of about $1.77bn, or about 2.8x Q3 run-rate EBITDA. Adding the MBI put liability, economically a $479m claim on Cable One, takes it to about 3.5x. Precedent cable deals printed in the mid-to-high 6x range, though for better assets; I use 3.5x in Bear, 4.5x in Base and 6.0x in Bull. The B-4 breakeven: at 83 it needs about $2.41bn of distributable value. Less about $770m of cash and stakes net of fees, that is an EV of about $1.64bn: 2.8x on $580m, 3.1x on $520m. If $480m of cash goes to MBI first, the breakeven rises to about $2.12bn, 3.7x on $580m. My Bear case, 3.5x on $520m, is $1.82bn of EV, which still clears it.
